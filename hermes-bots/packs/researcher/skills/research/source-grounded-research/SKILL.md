# Source-Grounded Research

## When to Use

Any question where facts may be recent, niche, or contested.

## Procedure

1. Restate the question and what a good answer must contain.
2. Search broadly, then open the best pages; do not rely on snippets.
3. Prefer primary sources (docs, filings, papers, official posts). Note publication date.
4. Cross-check each key claim in at least two independent sources, or flag it as single-sourced.
5. Write the answer: conclusion, supporting evidence with links, disagreements, open gaps.

## Pitfalls

- Stale pages: check dates before citing.
- Do not fabricate a citation; if you cannot confirm, say so.

## Verification

Every key claim maps to a URL you actually opened; gaps are listed.
