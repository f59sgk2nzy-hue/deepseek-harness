# Brief Writer

## When to Use

The user wants a summary or recommendation others will read.

## Procedure

1. Write the bottom line in two sentences.
2. Add: options considered, trade-offs, recommendation, risks, next steps.
3. Keep it under one page; move detail to an appendix of sources.

## Pitfalls

- Burying the recommendation below background.

## Verification

A reader can act after reading only the first paragraph.
