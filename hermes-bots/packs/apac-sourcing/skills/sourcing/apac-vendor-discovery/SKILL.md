# APAC Vendor Discovery

## When to Use

After an approved sourcing brief, to produce a long list of 10-25 candidate vendors.

## Procedure

1. Pick countries by fit, not habit. Typical strengths (verify per item):
   - China: broadest base for metals, fasteners, castings, forgings, rubber, plastics; highest tariff and UFLPA exposure.
   - Taiwan: precision fasteners, machined parts, tooling, electronics.
   - Japan and South Korea: high-spec steel, alloys, bearings, specialty chemicals; higher price, strong quality systems.
   - Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia: growing metalwork, rubber (Thailand, Malaysia), plastics, wire and cable; check for Chinese transshipment.
   - India: castings, forgings, machined parts, fasteners, chemicals.
   - Indonesia, Philippines: rubber, basic metals, assembly.
2. Search several channel types and record which channel found each vendor:
   - US import records (bill-of-lading data such as ImportYeti or Panjiva): shows who actually ships this product to US buyers. Strongest signal.
   - Industry associations and member directories (e.g. Taiwan Industrial Fastener Institute, national foundry and forging associations).
   - Trade-show exhibitor lists (Canton Fair, Fastener Fair Asia, and sector shows).
   - Certification databases: IAF CertSearch (ISO 9001) and IAQG OASIS (AS9100) searched by scope and country.
   - Marketplaces (Alibaba, Made-in-China, Global Sources, IndiaMART): leads only. "Verified" and "Gold" badges are paid listings, not vetting.
3. For each candidate capture: legal name (local and English), country/city, website, channel found, factory or trader (initial guess), relevant products, claimed certs, US customers seen in import data.
4. Drop obvious misfits with a one-line reason. Keep 10-25.

## Pitfalls

- Searching with the company's part numbers or drawings leaks confidential data. Search by generic material, process, and standard.
- One factory often appears under several trading-company names. Merge duplicates by address and phone.
- Ranking by marketplace position rewards ad spend, not capability.

## Verification

Each long-list entry has a source link and a channel; at least two non-marketplace channels were used.
