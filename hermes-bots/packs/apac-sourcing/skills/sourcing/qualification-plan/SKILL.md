# Qualification Plan

## When to Use

The buyer selected a vendor to pursue.

## Procedure

1. Write gates; each has an owner, cost estimate, duration, and pass criteria. The buyer approves passing each gate.
   1. Desk vetting complete (dossier, compliance screen green or cleared).
   2. NDA and, if needed, export-control review before sharing drawings.
   3. Samples with MTR/CoC; incoming inspection and, for critical items, independent lab testing (chemistry, mechanicals).
   4. Factory audit: on-site by staff or third party (QIMA, SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek), covering process control, calibration, traceability, subcontractors, and social compliance.
   5. FAI (AS9102) or PPAP as required; customer approval if the customer controls the source.
   6. Trial order with pre-shipment inspection (AQL plan stated), small quantity, protective payment terms.
   7. Steady state: scorecard review cadence, re-screening for sanctions and tariffs, dual-source plan.
2. Put a total timeline and cost on one table and flag the critical path.

## Pitfalls

- Skipping the factory audit because samples were good; golden samples are common.
- Large deposits before trial order passes.

## Verification

Every gate has owner, cost, duration, and pass criteria, and none moves without buyer approval.
