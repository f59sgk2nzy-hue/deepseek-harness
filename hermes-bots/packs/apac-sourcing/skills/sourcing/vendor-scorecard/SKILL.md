# Vendor Scorecard

## When to Use

After due diligence, compliance screens, and (when available) quotes and landed cost.

## Procedure

1. Score 1-5 with a one-line reason and evidence status for each criterion. Default weights (buyer may change):
   - Landed cost 25% · Quality system and spec capability 25% · Compliance risk 20% (any red = disqualify) · Capacity and lead time 10% · Financial and operational stability 10% · Communication and responsiveness 5% · Geographic and tariff diversification 5%.
2. Penalize unverified claims: score a VENDOR-CLAIMED item at most 3 until verified.
3. Show a ranked table, then a one-page memo: recommendation (primary and backup), why, main risks with mitigations, what would change the ranking.
4. End with "Decisions for you".

## Pitfalls

- Letting unit price dominate; landed cost and qualification risk decide most overseas switches.
- Recommending single-sourcing a critical aerospace item from one high-tariff country without a backup.

## Verification

Every score has a reason and evidence label; weights sum to 100%; disqualifications are explicit.
